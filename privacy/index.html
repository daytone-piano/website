DayTone privacy policy

Effective 19 October 2026

DayTone collects no data. It has no account, no tracking and no analytics, it sends nothing anywhere, and it works without an internet connection.

What DayTone collects

Nothing. DayTone does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data or usage data. It has no servers to send data to.

What DayTone stores on your device

One thing: whether you have seen the introduction shown on first launch, so that it is not shown again. It stays on your device and is deleted when you delete the app. DayTone keeps no history of your practice.

Network and permissions

DayTone needs no internet connection and makes no network requests. It asks for no permissions: no microphone, camera, location, contacts or notifications.

Third parties

DayTone includes no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking software. If you choose to share diagnostics with Apple or Google in your device settings, they may send crash reports to the developer under their own privacy policies.

This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics or trackers, and loads nothing from other sites. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log technical information such as IP addresses to keep the service secure, under the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Children

Because DayTone collects no data, it collects none from children either.
